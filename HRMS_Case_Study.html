HRMS System: eight modules, one employee lifecycle.

An enterprise-grade Human Resource Management System designed to streamline and automate the full employee lifecycle — from onboarding through exit — for mid-to-large organisations, covering recruitment, attendance, payroll, leave, performance and HR analytics in one role-based platform.

RoleUI UX Designer
CompanyEnterprise Product
DomainEnterprise · HRMS
ScopeEnd-to-end UX/UI, design system
01 — The challenge

Eight HR workflows, no shared system.

Onboarding, attendance, payroll, leave, performance and recruitment each ran as their own process for HR managers, payroll teams and employees — with no unified, role-based place to manage the full employee lifecycle.

Disconnected

Every HR function stood alone

Onboarding, attendance, payroll and performance review had no shared data or workflow, multiplying manual handoffs.

Role-blind

One view for every user

Admins, HR managers, team leads and employees needed very different information, not a single generic dashboard.

Field-unfriendly

Attendance built for desks, not sites

Marking attendance and applying for leave assumed a desk and a browser, not a phone in the field.

02 — Approach

Three decisions that shaped the system.

01

Eight modules, one platform

Onboarding, Attendance, Leave Management, Payroll, Performance Review, Recruitment, Employee Directory and HR Analytics were unified into a single role-based product instead of disconnected tools.

02

Role-based dashboards

Admin, HR Manager, Team Lead and Employee each get a dashboard scoped to what they actually need to see and act on.

03

Mobile-first for the field

Attendance marking and leave applications were designed mobile-first, so field employees aren't tied to a desk to check in or request time off.

03 — Inside the platform

The eight core modules.

Backed by a design system of 80+ HRMS-specific components for consistent UI across every module.

Onboarding

Structured new-hire flow

Guided onboarding steps replace scattered paperwork for new employees.

Payroll

Approval-chain processing

A payroll workflow with approval chains cut processing time by 45%.

Performance Review

Goals, self & manager feedback

Goal-setting, self-assessment and manager feedback flows in one review cycle.

HR Analytics

Attendance, leave & recruitment data

Recruitment, Employee Directory and HR Analytics round out the full employee lifecycle.
