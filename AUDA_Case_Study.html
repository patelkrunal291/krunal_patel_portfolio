Case Study · Government · Urban Planning

AUDA: four decades of urban planning, one citizen portal.

The official web portal for the Ahmedabad Urban Development Authority (AUDA) — established 1978 to plan and develop the physical infrastructure of the Ahmedabad region outside municipal limits. It brings plot booking, auditorium booking, property tax, tenders and emergency helplines into a single citizen-facing platform.

RoleUI UX Designer
CompanyAUDA — Govt. of Gujarat
DomainGovernment · Urban Planning
ScopeEnd-to-end UX/UI, portal design
01 — The challenge

Four decades of civic services, no digital front door.

AUDA plans and delivers urban infrastructure for millions of residents outside Ahmedabad's municipal limits — plot allotments, auditorium bookings, property tax, tenders — but each service historically meant a separate counter or process, with no single online home.

Counter-driven

Property services required office visits

Plot booking, property tax and the One Time Settlement Yojana all ran through in-person or paper processes.

Fragmented

Bookings split across venues

Auditorium bookings for Shela, Dehgam and Mahemdavad had no shared online system to check availability or reserve a date.

Hard to track

Tenders and certificates buried in notices

Authority certificates, project information and tender notices lived in scattered notifications with no structured, searchable home.

02 — Approach

Three decisions that shaped the portal.

01

One portal for every AUDA service

Home, About, News/Notifications, Gallery, Important Quick Links and Contact sections replaced scattered counters and notices with a single citizen-facing platform.

02

Booking and payment, moved online

The Auditorium Booking module (Shela, Dehgam & Mahemdavad) and AUDA Plot Booking facility, plus a One Time Settlement Yojana payment module, took property and venue transactions fully digital.

03

Structured access to civic information

Property Tax, Project Information Dashboard, Authority Certificate and Tender Notice modules gave residents a consistent, searchable way to track civic services.

03 — Inside the platform

What the portal actually covers.

Delivered as an accessible, mobile-responsive platform aligned with Government of Gujarat web standards.

Plot Booking

Residential & commercial plots

An online facility for booking AUDA plots, replacing in-person allotment processes.

Auditorium Booking

Three venues, one calendar

Shela, Dehgam and Mahemdavad auditoriums bookable through a shared online system.

OTS Yojana

One Time Settlement payments

Online payment module for property regularisation under the OTS Yojana.

Property Tax

Tax, tenders & certificates

Property Tax, Project Information Dashboard, Authority Certificate and Tender Notice modules in one place.
