International Kite Festival: one portal for a festival across ten cities.

The official web portal for Gujarat's International Kite Festival — one of India's most iconic cultural events, hosted annually in Ahmedabad since 1989 as part of the Uttarayan celebration. Built for the Tourism Corporation of Gujarat Limited (TCGL), it brings registration, schedules, venues and 50+ participating countries into a single tourism-grade experience.

RoleUI UX Designer
CompanyGujarat Tourism (TCGL)
DomainTourism · Government · Events
ScopeEnd-to-end UX/UI, event portal design
01 — The challenge

A nationwide festival with no single front door.

Master kite makers and flyers from 50+ countries converge on Gujarat every January, with events spread across 10+ cities and multiple venues along the Sabarmati Riverfront — but visitors had no single place to find schedules, locations or how to take part.

Fragmented

Venues scattered across the state

Events ran across Ahmedabad, Surat, Rajkot, Kutch, Saputara, Porbandar, Bhavnagar and more, with no city-wise way to find them.

International

50+ countries, one showcase needed

Participating countries needed a single, credible showcase — not scattered press releases and flyers.

Time-bound

A short, date-specific window

The whole festival runs across a handful of January days, so visitors needed schedules and travel planning info fast, not buried in static pages.

02 — Approach

Three decisions that shaped the portal.

01

A single portal for the whole festival

Home, About, Registration, Gallery, News, Planners and Contact sections replaced scattered flyers and press pages with one tourism-grade website.

02

A city-wise venue locator

A dedicated locator covers 10+ venues — Ahmedabad, Surat, Rajkot, Kutch, Saputara, Porbandar, Bhavnagar and more — so visitors could find their nearest event.

03

Countries showcase and a day-wise itinerary

A participating-countries showcase featuring 50+ nations sits alongside a day-wise itinerary module, so international flyers and local visitors could both plan around the same event calendar.

03 — Inside the platform

What the portal actually covers.

Built for both first-time visitors and returning kite-flying enthusiasts, with a mobile-first layout for the high volume of on-ground browsing during the festival.

10+ Cities

City-wise event locator

Ahmedabad, Surat, Rajkot, Kutch, Saputara, Porbandar, Bhavnagar and more, each with its own venue details.

50+ Countries

Participating countries showcase

India, USA, Japan, Germany, France, Brazil and dozens more kite-flying nations featured.

Free entry

No-registration visitor access
