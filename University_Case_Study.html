University Portal: one pattern, many institutions.

A reusable university portal pattern — academics, admissions, results and a Student Corner — deployed across a number of Gujarat and national institutions, including Sardar Patel University, Shri Govind Guru University, and the National Forensic Sciences University.

RoleUI UX Designer
CompanyGIPL / Govt. of Gujarat & India
DomainEducation · University Portals
ScopeEnd-to-end UX/UI, multiple institutions
01 — The challenge

Every university, its own disconnected experience.

Each institution ran its own mix of static pages, paper notices and counter-based processes for results, admissions and fee payment — with no shared, repeatable pattern for delivering a modern student experience across campuses.

Fragmented

Results lived in separate systems

Exam results, timetables and marksheets had no consistent student-facing home across institutions.

Paper-heavy

Notices and admissions offline

Admission notices, employment postings and academic council updates were scattered across counters and printed boards.

Hard to scale

No shared pattern to reuse

Each university needed its own build from scratch, with no common Student Corner, results or notice-board pattern to draw on.

02 — Approach

Three decisions that shaped the pattern.

01

A Student Corner as the shared anchor

A consistent Students Corner / student-dashboard pattern — results, timetables, fee payment — was reused and adapted across Sardar Patel University and Shri Govind Guru University.

02

Results and notices, standardized

Exam results, marksheets, Notice Boards and Academic Council updates followed the same structured modules at every institution, instead of one-off pages.

03

Scaled from a state university to a national institute

The same portal pattern flexed from a regional, multi-language SGGU deployment to NFSU's 13+ campus, Institution-of-National-Importance scale.

03 — A pattern that travels

Three examples, among several deployments.

This same Student Corner, results and notices pattern has shipped across a number of university and institute portals beyond these three — each adapting the core modules to its own scale and audience.

2021 · Vallabh Vidyanagar

Sardar Patel University

80+ UG/PG/PhD programmes with department-wise filtering, a Students Corner, and a high-volume Employment Notification module.

2022 · South Gujarat

Shri Govind Guru University

Student dashboards for exam results, timetables and fee payment, built multi-language-ready for tribal and rural communities.

2022 · 13+ Campuses

National Forensic Sciences University

The same pattern scaled to campuses across India and Uganda, with Notice Board, PhD Admissions and Academic Council modules.
