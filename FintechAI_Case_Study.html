Fintech AI Product: ask a question, get an underwriting-ready answer.

Credit Workbench is a conversational AI layer built on top of Databook — it turns a company name into a structured preliminary analysis, a scored risk view and a set of ready-to-run credit actions, all inside a single chat thread.

RoleDesign Lead
CompanyAccumn — A Yubi Company
DomainAI-assisted credit & risk
ScopeConversational UX, AI patterns
01 — The challenge

Turning a name into a lending decision is normally a research project.

The old path meant opening financials, ratings, litigation history and promoter data in separate tabs before a single judgment call. The brief was to compress that into a conversation — without the output reading like a generic chatbot answer.

Trust

AI answers have to earn credibility

An underwriter won't act on a paragraph of prose. Every AI response needed the same structured, sourced feel as Databook's own modules — tables, not just text.

Speed

Preliminary analysis eats hours

Company overview, financial trend and a red-flag summary normally means five open tabs before a first judgment call gets made.

Depth

One question hides many workflows

"Analyze this company" is really three jobs — check policy compliance, score the risk, decide whether to place a request — behind a single ask.

02 — Approach

Three decisions that shaped the product.

01

A search-first entry point

Before any chat starts, a personalized landing screen surfaces recently visited companies as one-click resumes and autocompletes new lookups by name, PAN or CIN — reopening yesterday's diligence never means retyping it.

02

One prompt, a structured brief

A single instruction like "Share a preliminary analysis" returns the same shape an analyst would build by hand: a Company Overview table and a Profit & Loss summary, plus a plain-language read on what's improving and what's still volatile.

03

Tools as the escalation path

Beyond free-form chat, a Tools menu exposes three purpose-built actions — Credit Risk Analysis, Credit Policy Check, Place Request — so a conversation can turn into a defined workflow without leaving the thread.

03 — Inside the product

A walk through the modules.

Four core surfaces from the shipped product, each turning a step of manual credit research into a click.

Module 01

Search Company

The landing screen greets the analyst by name and doubles as a workspace — recently visited companies sit one click away, and typing a name, PAN or CIN instantly filters to the right entity.

Module 02

Chat Interface

A single tap on a suggested prompt starts the thread; a lightweight "Analyzing data…" state keeps the wait legible while Databook's underlying records get pulled together behind the scenes.

Module 03

Results

The response lands as a Company Overview table and a Profit & Loss summary with trend callouts, followed by plain-language flags for what turned around and what's still inconsistent — skimmable in the time it takes to read a headline.

Module 04

Credit Tools

The Tools menu turns the same thread into a workbench — Credit Risk Analysis, Credit Policy Check and Place Request sit one click from any answer, so the next step never requires leaving the conversation.
